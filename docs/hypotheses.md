# Hypotheses

Each hypothesis follows the chain:
**I think** [pattern] **because** [reason] → **test** [comparison] → **if true** [action].
The Result column stays empty until the analysis is done (Day 13).

| # | Hypothesis | Why I believe it | How I'll test it | Decision if true | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Students who don't start in the first 14 days rarely complete | Habits form early; students who don't begin lose momentum | Completion rate by early-engagement group (0, <60, 60+ minutes in first 14 days) | Invest in onboarding and first-lesson reminders | |
| H2 | Engagement drops sharply in week 2 | The excitement of enrolling wears off before a routine forms | Number of active enrollments by week since enrollment | Send reminders at the end of week 1 | |
| H3 | One module in Data Science causes a large share of exits | Courses often have one hard topic (e.g. statistics) where weaker students give up | Module drop-off rate for each module, per course | Redesign that module; add practice and mentor support | |
| H4 | Students inactive for 14+ days rarely come back | The longer the break, the harder it is to restart | % of enrollments that resume after a 14+ day gap | Use 14 days of inactivity as the at-risk trigger | |
| H5 | Paid Social brings students with low completion and high refunds | Ads attract impulse buyers with lower commitment | Completion rate and refund rate by acquisition channel | Move marketing budget to better channels | |
| H6 | Live cohorts complete more than self-paced | A schedule and peers create accountability | Completion by batch type, checked within each course | Offer live cohorts for the hardest courses | |
| H7 | Course difficulty, not student location, explains completion gaps between city tiers | Students in big cities may simply choose harder courses | Compare city tiers overall, then within each course | Don't target a city tier for something the course causes | |
| H8 | Mobile learners complete less than desktop learners | Studying on a small screen is harder | Completion by device type | Improve the mobile experience | |

## Notes
- **H7** checks for a *confounder*: a hidden factor (course difficulty) that makes two groups look different when the real cause is something else.
- **H8** is expected to be **rejected**. Including a hypothesis that turns out false shows the analysis is honest, not just confirming what we wanted.
